FINAL YEAR RESEARCH — SEMICONDUCTOR PHYSICS

Strain-dependent performance of mono- and bilayer MoS₂ field-effect transistors.

The question was deceptively simple: how does stretching a two-dimensional semiconductor change the behaviour of a transistor?

I built and analysed a numerical device model in COMSOL, examining how tensile strain affects current characteristics in monolayer versus bilayer MoS₂ channels.

03 / HOW I WORK

AN ENGINEERING MINDSET

Curiosity,
with a method.

What interests me most is the space between an elegant model and a system that actually performs. I like understanding the physics, making the assumptions explicit, and testing whether the results make sense.

01

Ask a precise question.

Start with the problem, the constraints, and the physical or engineering mechanism worth investigating.

↗
02

Build an explainable model.

Choose equations, tools, and assumptions for a reason — not simply to produce a plausible-looking output.

↗
03

Compare, question, refine.

Check trends, challenge unexpected results, and relate the findings to established engineering literature.

↗
04

Communicate the value.

Turn technical insight into a clear explanation that researchers, engineers, and collaborators can build upon.
